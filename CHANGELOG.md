# Changelog

All releases of rs-css-theme, newest first. Each entry links its GitHub release page.

## v0.4.0 — 2026-11-21

Release: <https://github.com/abits/rs-css-theme/releases/tag/v0.4.0>

- The retrosafe look defines every token of the contract for light and dark mode through `light-dark()`.
- The look works in Vue, React, AlpineJS, Nuxt and Next, checked by host fixtures and unit tests.
- The demo in `demo/` previews the tokens of a look and switches between light, dark and system mode.
- The package is released under the MIT license.
